About me

A journey shaped by curiosity.

From limited learning opportunities to a deeper interest in mathematics.

Finding my way
through mathematics.

I grew up in an area where access to educational opportunities and modern learning resources was limited. Much of my early schooling relied on older textbooks and a traditional syllabus. Reaching beyond those resources took persistence and a willingness to learn independently.

Moving on to college at The Peace Group of Schools and Colleges in Abbottabad broadened my opportunities. I later completed my BS in Mathematics at COMSATS University Islamabad, where my interests developed toward algebra, number theory, and algebraic geometry.

My final-year project explored Elliptic Curve Cryptography. Alongside my degree, I studied algebraic geometry independently with guidance from my supervisor. I now hope to deepen this foundation through graduate study, while continuing to share mathematics through teaching.
