About me

A curiosity for structure.

A mathematics graduate with an interest in algebra, geometry, and the ideas that connect them.

My mathematical background

I completed my Bachelor of Science in Mathematics at COMSATS University Islamabad, Islamabad Campus, in 2025. My studies developed my foundation in abstract algebra, number theory, analysis, and computational mathematics.

My final-year project focused on Elliptic Curve Cryptography. Alongside my degree, I explored algebraic geometry under faculty guidance, building an interest in the relationship between geometric objects and algebraic structures.

Learning through teaching

Teaching is an important part of my academic experience. I have tutored Linear Algebra, Calculus, and Differential Equations, and conducted Python and LaTeX workshops through the SIAM CUI Student Chapter.

I also organized a small peer circle for graduate-study preparation, combining admission-test practice with research into programmes and application procedures.

Looking ahead

I am pursuing graduate-study opportunities in mathematics and continuing to develop my interests in algebraic geometry, number theory, and cryptography.
