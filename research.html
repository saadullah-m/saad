BS Mathematics · Final-year project

Elliptic Curve Cryptography

A study of the mathematical foundations of elliptic-curve-based cryptographic systems, supervised by Dr. Sohail Iqbal at COMSATS University Islamabad.

Read project report ↗

Project focus

The project explores elliptic curves over finite fields, their group structure, and how the difficulty of the Elliptic Curve Discrete Logarithm Problem underpins cryptographic applications.

  • Elliptic curves and the group law
  • Finite fields and the mathematical foundations of ECC
  • The Elliptic Curve Discrete Logarithm Problem
  • Applications to public-key cryptography
